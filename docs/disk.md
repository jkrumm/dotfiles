# Disk: the mini's Data volume, budgeted

The mini's Data volume is 894 GiB. The budget is data: `config/mini/disk-budget.yaml` has one row per path with an owner repo, a cap in GiB, a class and what enforces the cap. A daily job measures every row with `du -xk` and names the row that is over, so growth is found by its owner and not by the 93 % page.

## Budget

Caps are ceilings set above what each path measures today. `system_gib` (45) is the share no row covers: repos under 5 GiB, the rest of `~/.local`, swap, snapshots, and what `du` cannot read (Trash, Mail). The caps plus that share stay under 80 % of the volume; `make disk-budget-test` fails the commit that breaks it.

Classes: `data` is state to keep, `cache` regenerates, `build` is build output and worktrees, `history` is logs, backups and transcripts. The external SSD is not on this volume and has no row: `docs/external-ssd.md`, `docs/storage.md`.

## The check

`com.jkrumm.disk-budget` runs under jobrun at 06:15 daily, after the sweep. `make disk-budget-run` runs it once.

- **Status file** `~/.local/state/disk-budget/status.json`: every row with `measured_gib`, `cap_gib`, `over` and its signal, the volume's use, the commit sum, uncovered paths. The log is `~/Library/Logs/disk-budget.log`.
- **A row over its cap** files one factory signal on its owner, with the number and the cap. It files once, then stays quiet until the row has been under its cap again or 14 days pass. A repo the factory does not know gets the signal on dotfiles with the owner in the title.
- **Unknown growth:** any path over 5 GiB (`unknown_gib`) that no row covers files one signal on dotfiles. Add a row for it or remove what grows there.
- **The job fails** only on what a human edit repairs: a table that over-commits the volume, a signal the factory refused for the owner and for dotfiles alike. A row whose `du` stalls twice (300 s each) is `measured_gib: null` for that run and does not fail the job. The scan shares one 25 min deadline across its roots; the whole run stops measuring at 50 min (under the 3600 s jobrun timeout), and a row reached after that is unmeasured.
- **Changing a cap** is an edit of its row, then `make disk-budget-docs` (re-renders the table above) and `make disk-budget-test`. A cap that does not fit under the limit means another row has to give.

## Sweeps

`scripts/disk-sweep.sh` runs daily at 04:40 (`make disk-sweep-run` once); `com.jkrumm.disk-sweep-fstrim` runs its `colima-trim` every 4 h. Each step is one bounded rule and prints a `free space +N MB` line to `~/Library/Logs/disk-sweep.log`.

| Step | Removes |
|-|-|
| `tmpdir`, `node-cache` | top-level entries over 3 days in `$TMPDIR` and this user's `/private/tmp` that nothing holds open; stale node compile cache |
| `pkg-caches` | uv prune (forced when a `uv run` holds the lock), `brew cleanup -s`, npm over 3 GB, bun over 5 GB |
| `lima-cache`, `sim-runtimes`, `simulators` | Lima IPSWs a day old; all but the newest simulator runtime per platform, and the devices they orphaned |
| `worktrees` | `target/` and `node_modules/` of a closed Issue's factory worktree (source stays) |
| `seeds`, `scratch` | cargo seeds a day old except the current key's and the newest; `~/cache` dirs of closed Issues, idle ones over 14 days, sccache over 5 GB |
| `docker` | build cache over 5 GB, dangling and unused images over 48 h, anonymous volumes. Named volumes are only listed |
| `colima-trim` | `fstrim` returns pruned blocks to the host (20+ GiB regrow within hours) |

Claude Code prunes its own transcripts (`cleanupPeriodDays` 7, `config/settings.template.json`).

## When the alert fires

The alert is ClickStack's `Infra mini filesystem >= 93% (15m)` and the heartbeat's `disk` check at 93 %. The line stays at 93 %: issue 1026 panicked the mini at 94 % with thin swap.

1. `jq '.rows[] | select(.over)' ~/.local/state/disk-budget/status.json`, or `make disk-budget-run` for a fresh read. An over-cap row has an owner and a signal; the owner trims it.
2. No row over: a live build is the transient. A blend round in flight is three rounds (~73 GB) for ~2 h: `du -xsk ~/SourceRoot/weatherorb/products/weatherorb_blend/.building-*` grows ~7 MB/s; act only if it outlives its round.
3. Regenerable levers, in order: `make disk-sweep-run` (9 min; `fstrim` is the big one); `du -xsh ~/cache/*/seed/*` for a seed no `lsof` line names; `du -xsh ~/.software-factory/data/hygiene/*` for dirs `ps` does not name; `target/` of a closed Issue's worktree (`factory issue show`).
4. Never remove a live lead's `target/`, a named Docker volume, or anything on EXT.
5. A page that repeats means a cap is wrong or an enforcer is missing. Fix the row or its owner's rule, not the pager.

## Where things live

- **HomeLab:** the 2026-10 sweep moved personal media there; `hl copy homelab:ssd/Dokumente/mindsera_memos ~/Downloads/mindsera_memos` restores the memos. weatherorb's archive is the bucket `weatherorb-archive` (its ADR 0009).
- **Android SDK and emulator** (weatherorb `apps/mobile`) were removed; reinstall steps are in its README.
- **Agents cannot see `~/.Trash`, `~/Library/{Mail,Messages,Biome}` or `MobileSync`** (no Full Disk Access; `du` skips them silently, and the Trash once held 246 GiB). Measure through `ask-human ask '…' --cmd "ssh mini 'du -xhd1 ~/.Trash'"`.
- `docker system df` overstates reclaimable image space; the host only gets space back after `fstrim`.
- **Open:** the IU `prometheus-scripts` backup retention (stated 48 h + 30 d, 338 hourly and 212 daily dirs on disk) is that repo's and waits for the owner; its row is capped at the measured size. The mini's Photos library is gone and the nightly export is retired (`docs/storage.md`).
